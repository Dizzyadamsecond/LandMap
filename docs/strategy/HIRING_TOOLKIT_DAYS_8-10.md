## 30-MINUTE CALL GUIDE

### PRE-CALL PREP (10 min)
1. **Review candidate's full LinkedIn profile** + recent interviews/talks
2. **Identify 2-3 personal achievements** you'll reference (shows research)
3. **Prepare 3-4 specific LatAm regulatory/operational challenges** LandMap faces (context for them to think about)
4. **Know your answer to:** "Tell me about LandMap" (elevator pitch, 90 seconds)
5. **Have calendar open** for follow-up scheduling

### CALL FLOW (30 min)

**Opening (2 min):**
- "Thanks for 30 min. I know you're busy. I'll be concise."
- Reference something specific they've done (builds credibility)
- Example: "I saw your LeadDev talk on distributed teams—exactly what we need"

**Context (4 min):**
- Problem: "Real estate is $100B market in LatAm, 0% digital infrastructure"
- Solution: "LandMap = OS for property transactions. 73 routes live, $33M ARR, $1B valuation"
- Phase 1: "Need to scale from 50 → 120 people across 20 countries in 3 months"
- Ask: "Does this problem feel solvable to you?"

**Their Challenge (8 min):**
- "In your role at [Company], what was your biggest scaling challenge?"
- "How did you approach it?"
- "What would you do differently if you built it again?"
- Listen. Take notes. This tells you if they're strategic or tactical.

**LandMap's Specific Challenge (5 min):**
- "Here's our specific bottleneck: [regulatory/technical/operational challenge relevant to their expertise]"
- "How would you tackle it?"
- Pay attention: do they ask clarifying questions? Do they think like a builder?

**Opportunity (3 min):**
- "We're looking for a [title] to lead this. $1B valuation means equity is real wealth. Are you interested in exploring further?"
- **If YES:** "Great. Let's schedule a full pitch meeting with the CEO. When works?"
- **If NO/MAYBE:** "What would make this conversation more interesting?" (Don't push; let them guide)
- **If UNCLEAR:** "This feels relevant to your background. Can we do a follow-up call after you've thought about it?"

**Closing (2 min):**
- "Thanks for your time. Excited about this opportunity. Let's stay in touch."
- Send follow-up email with calendar invite for next step (if applicable)

**For Operations/COO candidates:**
- "You've scaled [company] to [size]. How did you think about hiring the first 100 people differently than 100-1000?"

**For Finance/CFO candidates:**
- "You've raised [amount] at [company]. How did you approach Series D/E fundraising?"

- "What's your framework for financial discipline at hypergrowth stage?"

**For Engineering candidates:**
- "You've scaled engineering from X → Y. Walk me through your hiring strategy for the first 50 engineers."
- "How do you maintain engineering culture across 4+ countries?"
- "What's the biggest technical scaling challenge you've faced?"

**For Government Affairs candidates:**
- "You've navigated regulation at [company]. How do you think about regulatory risk in new markets?"

**For Energy candidates:**
