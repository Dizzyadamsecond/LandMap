Zillow's valuation is $20B on ~$800M annual revenue. LandMap can achieve **$50B+ valuation on $2B+ annual revenue** if we own the infrastructure layer, not the transaction layer.

## ⚠️ RISKS & MITIGATION

### Risk 1: Regulatory Backlash (Medium)
- **Risk:** Governments restrict fractional ownership, token sales, energy trading
- **Mitigation:** VP Government Affairs on retainer; partnerships with chambers of commerce; gradual rollout with gov buy-in first
- **Impact if happens:** 6-month delay per country, revenue -20%

### Risk 2: Competitive Entry (High)
- **Risk:** Zillow/Redfin/Mercado Libre copy features, subsidize to kill us
- **Mitigation:** Move so fast they cannot catch up; own data moat (tile system) before competitors; 6-month speed advantage minimum
- **Impact if happens:** Revenue growth -30%, requires faster M&A strategy

### Risk 3: Macroeconomic Downturn (Medium)
- **Risk:** Real estate crash → transaction volume collapse → revenue -40%
- **Mitigation:** Diversify to non-transactional revenue (government, energy, AI data); government contracts provide stability
- **Impact if happens:** Mission unchanged, profitability timeline +12 months

### Risk 4: Energy Grid Regulations (Medium)
- **Risk:** Energy regulators (ANEEL Brazil, CNEE Mexico) restrict peer-to-peer energy trading
- **Mitigation:** Partner with large utilities first (not peer-to-peer); position as "grid efficiency tool" not disruptor
- **Impact if happens:** Revenue from energy systems -50%, still $100M+ ARR opportunity

### Risk 5: Execution (High)
- **Risk:** Team cannot execute 7 features in parallel across 6 countries
- **Mitigation:** Proven founders + hires from Airbnb, Uber, Stripe (successful scale-ups); weekly velocity tracking; bi-weekly all-hands on metrics
- **Impact if happens:** Delays Phase 2 by 4-8 weeks; requires hiring stronger engineering leadership
