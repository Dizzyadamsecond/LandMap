## ROLE SPECIFICATIONS & SEARCH CRITERIA

### 1. VP GOVERNMENT AFFAIRS
**Reports to:** CEO | **Base:** $180-250k | **Equity:** 0.5-1.2% | **Bonus:** 20-30% (gov relationships, regulatory wins)

**Why this matters for LandMap:**
- Land tokenization + renewable energy requires permitting in 20+ cities (Brazil, Mexico, Colombia)
- Government relationships = market access (municipal land registries, environmental clearance, tax incentives)
- VP Gov Affairs will report directly to CEO, lead relationships with state/federal energy agencies, coordinate compliance across jurisdictions

**Ideal candidate profile:**
- 10+ years in government relations (LatAm preferred)
- Track record: successfully lobbied for or negotiated with government agencies (energy, land, infrastructure sectors)
- Prior roles: gov relations at energy companies (Enel, Eletrobras, ex-PETROBRAS affiliates), land/real estate (Emaar, Heineken), or policy firms (lobbying firms in São Paulo, Mexico City, Bogotá)
- Languages: Portuguese + Spanish minimum
- Networks: established relationships with energy ministry officials, state governors, municipal planners
- Current/recent: VP Gov Affairs at energy/infrastructure companies OR director-level at top 3 Latin American lobbying firms

**Search platforms & keywords:**
- LinkedIn: `"government affairs" OR "public affairs" OR "government relations" + (Brazil OR Mexico OR Colombia) + (energy OR real estate OR infrastructure) + (VP OR Director OR C-level)`
- AngelList: filter "Investor Relations" + "Government Affairs" roles, LATAM focus
- Justminded (LatAm lobbying directory): search SP/Mexico City/Bogotá firms, top 50
- Executive search: target ex-government officials (ex-Ministry of Energy staff who moved to private sector)

**Red flags to avoid:**
- No LatAm market experience (US-only lobbying won't translate)
- Not fluent in Portuguese or Spanish
- Experience only in regulated utilities (need someone who's navigated startup/emerging sectors too)

**First conversation hooks:**
- "We're building a land tokenization market in 20+ cities across LatAm. Gov access is how we win. Have you navigated renewable energy permitting at scale?"
- "Your work at [Company] on [Specific Policy] is exactly the playbook we need for municipal land registry access."

## WARM INTRO PROCESS

**Step 1: Identify connection**
- Check LinkedIn: "People also viewed" or "This person is connected to [Mutual connection]"
- Check your network: ask 3-5 people "Do you know [Candidate]?"
- Check your investor/board list: do they know candidate?

**Step 2: Craft warm intro request**
```
Subject: Intro: [Candidate Name] — VP Gov Affairs opportunity

Hi [Mutual connection],

I'm hiring a VP Government Affairs for LandMap (renewable energy + land tokenization in LatAm). 
[Candidate Name]'s work at [Company] on [Specific project] is exactly what we need.

Would you be comfortable introducing us? I can send context on the role.

Thanks!
```

**Step 3: Wait for response**
- Give 2-3 days before follow-up
- If no response, try different angle (LinkedIn message directly to mutual connection, not email)

**Step 4: Intro format (if they agree)**
```
CC: [Candidate Name]

[Mutual connection]: happy to intro [Candidate Name] to [You], who's hiring a VP Gov Affairs role at LandMap.

[You]: [Candidate Name] led [Specific work] at [Company] — exactly the background we need.

Let me know if you want to chat!
```

**Step 5: Direct follow-up (after warm intro)**
- Send personalized note to candidate within 24h
- Reference mutual connection + specific work you admire
- Propose 20-min call

## INTERVIEW GUIDE

### Round 1: Intro Call (20 min)
**Your goals:**
1. Confirm fit on paper (background, experience, motivation)
2. Assess cultural fit (values, communication style)
3. Gauge interest level (open to role? Timeline?)
4. Give 2-minute pitch on LandMap (problem, opportunity, why them)

**Key questions:**
- "Tell me about your proudest professional accomplishment."
- "What are you optimizing for in your next role?" (growth, impact, compensation, location, team?)
- "Why does LandMap excite you?" (or "What questions do you have?")
- "What's your timeline for a move?" (now, 3 months, 6 months?)

### Round 2: Deep dive (60 min)
**Your goals:**
1. Assess technical/functional expertise
2. Evaluate decision-making process (case study or past scenario)
3. Check team collaboration + leadership
4. Explore regulatory/market knowledge (context on role)

**Sample questions by role:**

**VP Gov Affairs:**
- "Walk me through a lobbying campaign you led. What was the goal? How did you approach gov relationships?"
- "How do you navigate conflicts between founder vision and regulatory constraints?"
- "What's your experience with LatAm government processes? Any challenges?"

**CFO:**
- "Take me through a Series A fundraise you led. What was hard? What would you do differently?"
- "How do you build a unit economics model for a marketplace? Walk me through it."
- "Describe a time you had to deliver bad news to investors. How did you handle it?"

**COO:**
- "Tell me about a multi-city ops launch. What did you get wrong the first time? How did you fix it?"
- "How do you build vendor/supplier relationships at scale? Any mistakes?"
- "What's the most important operational metric to track for a marketplace?"

**VP Engineering:**
- "Describe your experience scaling an engineering team. How many engineers? What was hardest?"
- "How do you think about technical debt vs shipping velocity?"
- "Tell me about an infrastructure project you led at scale. QPS? Latency requirements?"

**VP Energy Systems:**
- "What's your approach to standardizing solar ROI calculations across regions?"
- "Tell me about government relationships you've built in energy sector. How do they help the business?"
- "How would you build credibility with institutional energy investors?"

### Round 3: Offer stage (if interested)
**CEO to candidate:**
- "Are you excited about the role? What would make it a yes?"
- "Any compensation questions or concerns?"
- "What's your ideal start date?"
- "Any references or background check items we should know about?"

If yes:
- Send offer letter (within 24 hours)
- Include equity details, vesting schedule, performance milestones
- Timeline: 3-5 day response expected (get signature or decline)
